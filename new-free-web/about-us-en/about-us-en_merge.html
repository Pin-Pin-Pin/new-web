About Us

Citizen of the Earth, Taiwan (CET) is a local environmental protection foundation in Taiwan. Our team consists of environmental practitioners with more than twenty years of experience in Taiwan.

Our Vision

CET is dedicated to achieving environmental sustainability in Taiwan and tackling the challenges of climate change through direct action. Our mission is to raise awareness within Taiwanese civil society and inspire a willingness to take action. Ultimately, we aim to empower everyone to become 'Citizens of the Earth.'

Our Approach

We employ diverse strategies in our campaigns to protect the planet and promote civic participation in environmental decision-making. These range from non-institutional protests to institutional actions such as environmental litigation and policy advocacy.

Our actions are firmly rooted in investigating, researching, monitoring, and exposing environmental issues to develop and propose effective solutions. To achieve this, we collaborate closely with environmental organizations, local groups, residents, scholars, and experts.

To popularize environmental awareness, we actively publish editorials and books, host engaging events and lectures, and actively manage social media channels to foster direct conversations with the public.
